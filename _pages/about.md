[**Project**](https://github.com/Ethan-Zheng136/Map-Uncertainty-for-Trajectory-Prediction)

- I carried out experiments, code implementation, and visualization. This work addresses mapless trajectory prediction in autonomous driving by modeling map uncertainty with covariance and introducing a proprioceptive scenario gating mechanism driven by the ego vehicle's kinematic state. The method improves prediction accuracy and robustness across diverse driving scenarios, achieving up to a 23.6% performance gain on nuScenes compared with existing approaches.
